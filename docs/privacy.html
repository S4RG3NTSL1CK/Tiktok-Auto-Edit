Tiktok Auto Edit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Tiktok Auto Edit is a free, open-source desktop application built and maintained by an individual developer. It runs entirely on your own computer — there is no company, no backend server, and no account system operated by the app itself.

What data is stored, and where

API keys and login tokens for the services you choose to connect (Freesound, Jamendo, AudD, and TikTok) are stored only on your own computer, in your operating system's standard per-application configuration folder. They are never transmitted to the developer, because there is no server for them to be sent to.

What data is transmitted, and to whom

The app makes network requests directly from your computer to the third-party services you choose to use, using your own credentials for each:

  • Freesound, Jamendo, AudD — to search for or check music/audio, using your own API key for that service.
  • TikTok — only if you connect your account. The app requests the video.upload and user.info.basic scopes, used solely to upload a video clip you explicitly select to your own TikTok account's draft inbox, and to display your account name in the app so you can confirm you're connected to the right account. The app does not read, store, or share your TikTok content, followers, messages, or any other account data.
  • GitHub — to check for and download app updates from this project's public release page.

Each of these is a direct connection between your computer and that service, governed by that service's own privacy policy. The app itself does not operate any server, does not collect analytics, does not track usage, and does not sell or share data with anyone, because it has no mechanism to collect anything in the first place.
